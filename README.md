# Business Insights 360 Dashboard

## 📌 Project Description
Business Insights 360 is an end-to-end Business Intelligence dashboard developed using Power BI to analyze and monitor organizational performance across Finance, Sales, Marketing, Supply Chain, and Executive operations.  

The project integrates multiple datasets from Excel files and transforms raw business data into interactive dashboards and KPI-driven reports for better decision-making. The dashboard enables users to analyze revenue, profitability, customer performance, product trends, regional sales, and supply chain efficiency through dynamic visualizations and filters.

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- Develop a centralized business intelligence solution for monitoring business performance.
- Analyze financial metrics such as Net Sales, Gross Margin, and Net Profit.
- Track customer, product, and regional performance using interactive dashboards.
- Improve business decision-making through KPI analysis and data visualization.
- Monitor supply chain forecasting accuracy and inventory risks.
- Build an executive-level dashboard for consolidated business insights.

---

## 📊 Key Insights Generated
- Identified top-performing products, customers, and regions based on revenue and profitability.
- Analyzed sales trends and year-over-year business growth.
- Evaluated Gross Margin and Net Profit across multiple business segments.
- Monitored operational expenses and their impact on profitability.
- Tracked forecast accuracy, net error, and inventory risks in the supply chain.
- Compared actual business performance against benchmarks and targets.
- Analyzed revenue contribution by channels, divisions, and market segments.
- Enabled executives to monitor KPIs through a centralized interactive dashboard.

## 📊 Dashboard Views

### 💰 Finance View
Analyzes key financial metrics including Net Sales, Gross Margin, Operational Expenses, and Net Profit. Helps track overall financial performance against benchmarks and targets.

### 📈 Sales View
Provides insights into customer performance, regional sales trends, and revenue contribution across different markets and business segments.

### 📣 Marketing View
Focuses on product performance analysis by evaluating sales growth, profitability, and product category contribution through interactive visualizations.

### 🚚 Supply Chain View
Monitors Forecast Accuracy, Net Error, inventory risks, and supply chain efficiency to identify stock-related issues and operational gaps.

### 👨‍💼 Executive View
Offers a high-level overview of the business by consolidating KPIs and insights from all departments into a single interactive dashboard for executives and stakeholders.
